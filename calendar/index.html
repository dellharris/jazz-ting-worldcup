The agenda

Three points for the call.

01

Creator membership offer

A discount or partnership program so IDU House creators can join MyMP3Pool at a better rate and start getting records to DJs.

Artist placement partnership

A formal route for IDU House artists to place songs on the MyMP3Pool platform, with clean versions and metadata handled up front.

IDU House Sounds radio program

A recurring radio program, IDU House Sounds, presented with MyMP3Pool and built around new independent music and the DJs who break it.

  • Format, cadence and hosting
  • Featured artist and DJ segments
  • Cross promotion on both platforms
