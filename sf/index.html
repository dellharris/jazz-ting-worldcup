Southern Frequency is a live hi-fi beat experience — Dell Harris on the SP-404, threading jazz-inflected production, Southern rap, mellow grime, and deep sample culture into a single unbroken listening session. Warm bass. Analog texture. The kind of music that makes a room go still.

Dell Harris is an Atlanta-based DJ, producer, and sample artist. His sound lives at the intersection of MF DOOM–era abstract rap, Memphis and ATL underground, and the mellow grime of late-night London — heard through a serious hi-fi system. Southern Frequency is where that world gets performed live.
